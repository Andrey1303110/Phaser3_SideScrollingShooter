# Campaign ability effects

The ImageGen master `effects.png` supplies the two projectiles, breath stream, impact flare, shield, broken shield, healing spiral and experience emblem. The small traveling mote comes from the healing artwork. Run `node art/source/skill-effects/pack.cjs` with `sharp` available to rebuild `src/assets/sprites/skill_fx.png` and its Phaser atlas.

Saturated clear color shapes plus localized soft edge bloom, strong whites inside, crisp shards, superb motion-design draftsmanship. Each asset must remain clear as a small thumbnail.
